### Evidence-derived research anchors

- **Whale combinations:** Sharma et al., *Nature Communications* (2024), [contextual and combinatorial structure of sperm-whale codas](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-024-47221-8). Rhythm, tempo, rubato and ornamentation are distinguishable properties; there is no established translation of every coda.
- **Dolphin identity:** Janik et al., [signature whistle identity](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1472465/); [signature whistle contour analysis](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0077671); [copied whistle addressing in wild dolphins](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3740840/). The method must separate the whistle's learned shape, copying behavior, speaker and actual communicative meaning.
- **Field acoustic monitoring:** [Practical guide to automatic bioacoustic detection](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11885706/). Recorder groups, background noise, labeling, sampling and held-out validation constrain detector claims; do not infer animal psychology from raw signals.
- **Animal deception:** Whiten and Byrne, [tactical deception in primates](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/behavioral-and-brain-sciences/article/abs/tactical-deception-in-primates/100E1747829535A834C21FB6D361D00C). Behavioral descriptions and alternate non-deceptive explanations must stay distinct from intentional-lying attribution.
- **SETI method falsification:** [Analysis of blc1 and technosignature verification controls](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41550-021-01508-8). Off-source controls, known RFI, instrument health and independent follow-up are necessary checks; the absence of a terrestrial explanation never proves alien origin.
- **Sensitive endangered taxa:** [GBIF best practices](https://docs.gbif.org/sensitive-species-best-practices/master/en/). Generalize coordinates or withhold altogether according to custodian sensitivity categories. No exact location is accepted by the V27 release gate.
- **Dinosaur/fossil chronology:** [Smithsonian dating overview](https://humanorigins.si.edu/evidence/dating). Relative stratigraphy, radiometric uncertainty and fossil age must not be conflated. [Fitch (1971)](https://academic.oup.com/sysbio/article-abstract/20/4/406/1673276) counts minimum character changes on a *predetermined phylogenetic topology*; it cannot directly prove true ancestral taxa.
- **Quantum requirement:** [NIST Quantum Sensing Explained](https://www.nist.gov/quantum-information-science/quantum-sensing-explained). Atomic clocks, magnetometers and single-photon detectors have physical implementation obligations. A classical simulation or noise figure is not evidence of quantum advantage.
- **Three-point analogy:** [NBA three-point rules](https://jr.nba.com/3-point-shot/): three points for a successful attempt behind the arc. Two- versus three-point *expected value* is a simple finite abstraction, not real-game decision science.
- **Cryptographic chalkboard:** repeat exact text with position-bound SHA-256 digests. Hashes reveal changes across a supplied ordered sequence; they **do not** establish who wrote the lines or make copied statements independent.

## Eight actual bounded local functions

`lib/mpc-v27-space-lab.mjs` contains:

1. `compareSymbolicCallSequenceV27`: adjacent-symbol mutual information (MI) and deterministic Fisher-Yates shuffled null controls over **5–64 supplied symbolic labels** and **8–128 permutations**. Empirical permutation tail fractions describe only the declared finite symbolic sequence; neither recording independence, animal language, species, meaning nor caller attribution is inferred. No audio capture or live organism action.
2. `fitchTreeParsimonyV27`: bounded rooted binary tree with **≤31 nodes**, **≤16 leaves** and A/C/G/T tip characters. Compute Fitch minimum unordered state changes and possible root character sets for the **given topology only**. Validate unique parents, root reachability and no unsupported character states.
3. `bracketFossilLayerAgeV27`: use independently supplied above/below layer geological ages (Ma) and reported margins to derive a **broad possible age interval**, flag nominally reversed layers and overlapping or impossible brackets. Never claim the specimen was directly dated or species identified.
4. `boundSpaceSignalTravelV27`: use `c=299792.458 km/s` to calculate vacuum one-way and round-trip distance lower bounds from declared distance intervals, and flag a supplied round trip shorter than that physical bound. This is not spacecraft telemetry, quantum communication, or a clock authentication check.
5. `auditSetiCandidateControlsV27`: finite on/off source, instrument validity, known RFI and repeat-observation Boolean/UNKNOWN gate, without invoking telescopes or declaring technosignatures.
6. `gateSensitiveTaxonReleaseV27`: accepts a **sensitivity category and disclosure precision policy**, never raw coordinates. Uses GBIF-like conservative metadata disclosure guard: EXTREME and UNKNOWN withheld, HIGH ≤1 fractional-degree digit, MEDIUM ≤2, LOW ≤3, NOT_SENSITIVE ≤6. This **does not** validate the classification owner or audit every nested field in a real occurrence database; real release must stay under provider policy.
7. `compareThreePointExpectedValueV27`: simple declared `2 × p(two)` versus `3 × p(three)`; threshold `p(three) = 2p(two)/3`. No observed NBA player, foul, possession or shooting accuracy claims.
8. `auditRepeatedDigestChainV27`: indexed SHA-256 digest chain over at most 32 short supplied lines; returns only digests and count/unique count, not the original line text. Repetition is not independent evidence, and a chain digest is not a signed/authenticated audit log.

`lib/mpc-v27-method-space-router.mjs` implements `routeSpaceOfMethodsV27`: select an **explicit domain**, source/owner/version-bound atom batch ≤64, ≤8 scoped research contracts, separate primary missing records from hypothetical unexecuted outputs, preserve source-version disagreements and zero-delta STOP, offer independent falsifier/mirror questions, and permit only typed/same-domain or explicit META research interfaces. No research method is executed by selection. ## Method on method: serious non-equivalences and cross-examination

```text
Observed animal audio
  != classified symbol sequence
  != acoustic structure
  != caller identity
  != communicative meaning

SETI instrument reading
  != candidate signal
  != terrestrial RFI eliminated
  != re-observed independent signal
  != extraterrestrial origin

Fossil-bearing layer
  != direct fossil age
  != Fitch model's possible ancestral character
  != actual common ancestor

Quantum measurement label
  != quantum hardware used
  != matched classical control
  != proven quantum advantage

Known endangered species presence
  != permission to disclose exact occurrence
  != safe public map coordinates
```

The novel transfer is the **method-space comparison** itself: a single signal-detection falsifier can inform whether to request additional controls in different disciplines, while **never equating the objects studied**. Identity, authenticity, uncertainty, observer bias, sampling completeness, source provenance and harm all remain separate branches.
