# MPC UNIVERSAL DOCTRINE + CROSS-BORDER METHOD BRIDGE
## UMTB-2.0 — INTELLIGENCE / CYBER / CONSTITUTIONAL / NASA / SPACE / FIRST-PRINCIPLES EXTENSION

This extension is ADDITIVE to UMTB-1.0.

It does not replace:

- canonical MAXVAR 1–256;
- MBSS;
- BL-SOLID-STATE;
- NESTMAX;
- the Universal Evidence Contract;
- existing implemented evaluators;
- existing method limitations.

Its purpose is to make high-value methods discovered in one discipline transferable into another discipline without pretending that the originating discipline has authority over the destination domain.

---

# 1. CENTRAL RULE — METHOD TRANSFER WITHOUT AUTHORITY TRANSFER

A method may transfer.

The originating institution's authority does not.

Examples:

CIA ACH can analyze competing hypotheses in a bug-bounty investigation.

That does NOT turn the bug-bounty investigation into intelligence activity.

NASA requirements traceability can organize a contract analysis.

That does NOT make NASA standards legally binding on the contract.

NSA Zero Trust concepts can improve evidence validation.

That does NOT make an NSA cybersecurity guideline a rule of evidence.

Space Force course-of-action comparison can structure alternative research paths.

That does NOT convert research into military planning.

Tesla first-principles engineering can challenge assumptions.

That does NOT make an attributed Elon Musk practice an authoritative engineering standard.

Use:

METHOD_TRANSFER = permitted when structural fit exists.

AUTHORITY_TRANSFER = prohibited unless independently established.

# 4.4 ACH — ANALYSIS OF COMPETING HYPOTHESES

Default high-value intelligence method.

Use where at least two plausible explanations exist.

```text
H1
H2
H3
...

E1 -> supports / contradicts / neutral / unknown each hypothesis
E2 -> ...
```

Prefer DISCONFIRMING evidence over merely accumulating confirming material.

Ask:

```text
Which evidence is most diagnostic?

Which evidence fits every hypothesis and therefore proves little?

What evidence should exist if H1 were true?

What evidence should exist if H2 were true?

What expected evidence is absent?

Could denial, deception, logging gaps, stale documentation,
or observation-window limits explain the absence?
```

ACH must retain weaker hypotheses when unresolved.

ACH count != probability.
